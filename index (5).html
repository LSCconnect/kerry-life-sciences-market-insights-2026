Where the demand is

Mid and senior level, and growing

This isn't entry-level work. There's a strong and immediate need for experienced engineers and specialists, and it falls into two main tracks.

Sterile fill-finish and lyophilisation project delivery

Project Managers, Technical SMEs and Project Engineers who can take process equipment packages from design review through FAT and SAT, installation and qualification.

Technology transfer and new product introduction

People who can bring sterile injectable products into a new site: process transfer, the validation interface, analytical and QC method transfer, and GMP documentation and compliance.

Your CV

For these roles, detail wins

"Worked on fill-finish" won't carry a CV here. Employers want to see exactly what you did, on what equipment, and at which stage of the project.

  • Name the equipmentMakes, models and vendors of the fillers, lyophilisers, washers, isolators, cappers or inspection systems you've worked on.
  • Show what you owned, and whenBe clear about your part across construction, commissioning and qualification, and handover to QA.
  • Spell out your project deliveryDesign reviews, FAT and SAT, vendor management, and any travel to vendor sites in Europe or the US.
  • Cover the last fifteen years, with no gapsFor senior technical roles, two pages is rarely enough. Walk through each role, your responsibilities, the projects you contributed to and what you achieved.
  • Explain it plainlySay what you did, why you made the decisions you made, and what the outcome was, in a way someone outside your discipline can follow.
We can tailor your CV to fit the role, but if the information is not there, it becomes much harder to present your background in the strongest possible light, no matter how relevant your experience actually is.Stephen Abdullah, Senior Technical Resourcer, LSC
The skills that keep coming up

What employers in Kerry are asking for

Process & CQV

  • End-to-end CQV ownership from FAT and SAT through IQ, OQ, PQ and handover
  • URS, impact assessments, FMEA, DQ, protocols and reports
  • Risk-based qualification and deviation or CAPA leadership
  • Vendor and OEM management through delivery milestones
  • Fill-finish lines, lyophilisers, compounding, CIP/SIP, single-use systems and visual inspection

CSV & digital systems

  • CSV lifecycle delivery across plans, URS, risk assessments, IQ/OQ/PQ and summary reports
  • GAMP 5, ALCOA+ data integrity and 21 CFR Part 11 or Annex 11
  • Validation of automated platforms used in sterile manufacturing
  • AI or vision systems, where relevant
  • Automation platforms such as MES/SCADA and related systems

Tech transfer & NPI

  • Process transfer and PPQ
  • Method transfer and analytical or QC transfer
  • ICH Q2 requirements
  • Transfer protocols and the validation interface
  • GMP documentation and compliance through transfer

What makes a profile stand out

  • Hands-on sterile fill-finish and aseptic manufacturing experience
  • Practical Annex 1 and GMP or data integrity understanding
  • Greenfield, start-up or multi-supplier integration experience
  • Willingness to travel to vendor sites and adapt to the site's changing onsite requirements
